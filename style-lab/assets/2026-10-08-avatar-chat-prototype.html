Rebuilding checkout for first-time shoppers

Prototype: click Ron's avatar in the corner to open his aside.

Context

New shoppers were abandoning the cart at the payment step. Session recordings showed people scrolling up and down the page, looking for the total and the promo code field before committing.

What we changed

We moved the order summary next to the payment form, collapsed optional fields, and reduced checkout to a single screen. Completion rose in the first two weeks after launch.
